# About Notes


## What is Notes?

Notes is a personal archive for recording thoughts, experiments, and discoveries.

It collects ideas around design, technology, creative projects, and the process of learning.


## The Idea Behind Notes

Not every idea needs to become a finished product.

Sometimes a small observation, an unfinished experiment, or a simple question can lead to unexpected discoveries.

Notes is a place to preserve these fragments — a space where ideas can grow through exploration and reflection.


## What I Explore

Notes focuses on:

- design and visual experiments
- technology and digital tools
- creative processes
- observations and ideas


## About OhHoBa

OhHoBa is a creative project exploring language, symbols, and imagination.

This blog is where I record the thoughts, experiments, and discoveries behind my creative journey.
