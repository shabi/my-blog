OhHoBa is an experimental space dedicated to exploring playful languages, unconventional writing, and curious symbols.

Here, words are treated not only as tools of communication, but also as objects of imagination and design.

Through articles, experiments, and observations, OhHoBa explores the hidden possibilities within language and symbols.
