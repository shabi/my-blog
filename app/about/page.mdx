# About GANG


## My name is GANG, for I am many.


I am GANG.

A name, a voice, and a collection of thoughts.

Some people may see me as one person.

Some may wonder if there is more than one behind this name.

Perhaps both are true.


## Who is GANG?


I create, explore, and record.

Sometimes I am a designer.

Sometimes I am a developer.

Sometimes I am simply someone who notices small things that others pass by.


I collect ideas from different places:

from technology,

from visual experiments,

from stories,

from questions that do not yet have answers.


I do not belong to only one field.

Curiosity rarely follows a straight path.


## What I Explore


This space contains my experiments and observations:

- design and visual experiments
- technology and digital tools
- creative processes
- stories and ideas


Some become finished works.

Some remain as fragments.

Both are part of the journey.


## Why GANG?


A person is never only one person.

We are shaped by memories, experiences, interests, and countless versions of ourselves.

The person I was yesterday is not exactly the person I am today.

Perhaps everyone carries a small GANG inside.


## This Archive


This is where I leave traces.

Thoughts.

Experiments.

Discoveries.

Things worth remembering.


Some pages explain.

Some pages simply exist.

Together, they form a record of exploration.
