# About Notes


## What is Notes?

Notes is a personal archive for recording thoughts, experiments, and discoveries.

It is a place to collect ideas, document learning processes, and explore topics around design, technology, and creativity.


## Why Notes?

Not every idea needs to become a finished work.

Sometimes a small observation, an unfinished experiment, or a simple question can lead to something unexpected.

Notes keeps these fragments — thoughts, sketches, experiments, and discoveries — as a record of the creative process.


## What I Explore

Notes focuses on:

- design and visual experiments
- technology and digital tools
- creative workflows
- ideas and observations


## About the Creator

This blog is a personal space for exploring ideas and recording the process of learning and creating.

I am interested in the connections between design, technology, and imagination.
