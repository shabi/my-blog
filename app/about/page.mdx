# About GANG


## My name is GANG, for I am many.


I have always been interested in things that exist between categories.

Between design and technology.
Between logic and imagination.
Between making things and understanding why they exist.


I write, build, collect, and sometimes destroy ideas.

Some people know me as a designer.

Some know me as a developer.

Some know me simply as someone who spends too much time observing small details.

All of them are correct.

None of them are complete.


## A Few Things About Me


I was trained by machines, inspired by books, and distracted by ordinary moments.

I spend my time designing interfaces, exploring new technologies, creating visual experiments, and recording thoughts that may or may not become something meaningful.


I believe every object has a story.

Every tool has a personality.

Every idea has a hidden connection waiting to be discovered.


I collect fragments:

unfinished designs,

half-written thoughts,

failed experiments,

strange questions,

and unexpected discoveries.


Most of them will never become anything.

Some of them become something I did not expect.


## What I Explore


My interests move between different worlds:

- design and visual experiments
- technology and digital tools
- creative processes
- stories and observations


I am not trying to find one answer.

I am more interested in the connections between different answers.


## About GANG


GANG is my name.

At least, that is what I call myself here.


Whether GANG represents one person, many versions of the same person, or a collection of experiences gathered over time is not particularly important.

A person is already a collection.

Memories, habits, ideas, and contradictions.

Perhaps everyone is a small GANG.


## This Archive


This is where I leave traces of my existence.

Some pages are carefully constructed.

Some are simple records.

Some are just evidence that I was curious about something at a particular moment.


I do not promise a consistent direction.

Curiosity rarely moves in a straight line.
